# Billing Document: Refund Processing After Service Cancellation

**Document ID:** BILL-0008
**Category:** Billing
**Applies To:** Comcast Residential and Business Accounts
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-07-18

## Summary
Reference guide for support agents explaining refund processing after service cancellation, including how to interpret the relevant billing system fields and how to communicate this clearly to customers.

## Explanation
Refund Processing After Service Cancellation is one of the most common billing inquiry categories handled by Tier 1 support. Agents should review the customer's billing statement in the CRM billing module before responding, focusing on the line items relevant to refund processing after service cancellation.

### Key Billing System Fields
- **Account Balance:** Current outstanding amount including any past-due balance.
- **Current Charges:** Charges for the upcoming billing cycle, including recurring service fees, equipment rental, and one-time charges.
- **Adjustments/Credits:** Any manual credits applied by agents, which must include a reason code and approving agent ID.
- **Payment History:** Last 12 months of payment transactions, including method and status.

## Agent Guidance
1. Verify the customer's identity before discussing any billing details.
2. Pull up the specific billing cycle in question and identify the line item(s) related to refund processing after service cancellation.
3. Explain the charge or policy in plain language, avoiding internal billing system jargon.
4. If the customer disputes a charge, determine whether it falls within the standard adjustment authority (typically up to $15 without supervisor approval).
5. Apply any approved credit or adjustment directly in the billing system and provide the customer with a confirmation number.
6. If the inquiry involves a recurring or systemic billing error affecting multiple customers, log a billing systems ticket for the Billing Operations team.

## Common Customer Questions
- "Why did my bill increase this month?" — Check for promotional rate expiration, plan changes, or added equipment fees.
- "What is this equipment fee?" — Explain the monthly rental charge for ISP-owned Comcast equipment such as the router/ONT/modem, and mention the option to purchase compatible equipment to remove this fee.
- "Can I get a refund?" — Refunds are processed for confirmed billing errors or service outages exceeding the SLA-defined threshold, per the Refund Processing SOP.

## Escalation Rules
Disputes exceeding the agent's adjustment authority, or involving a customer threatening regulatory complaint, must be escalated to a Billing Supervisor within 1 business hour per Escalation Policy ESC-0009.
