# Why is my IPv6 connectivity not working (Fixed Wireless)

**Document ID:** FAQ-0069
**Category:** Frequently Asked Questions
**Applies To:** Airtel Fixed Wireless Service, VoIP ATA
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-11-14

## Summary
This FAQ addresses common customer questions regarding VPN tunnel not establishing on Airtel Fixed Wireless connections using a VoIP ATA. It is intended for Tier 1 support agents and self-service customers.

## Question
Why is my IPv6 connectivity not working (Fixed Wireless)?

## Answer
Customers experiencing VPN tunnel not establishing on their VoIP ATA should first verify the physical layer before assuming a service outage. In most cases the underlying cause is related to an expired or revoked TR-069 (CWMP) provisioning certificate on the ACS server.

### Step-by-Step Guidance
1. Confirm all cabling (Ethernet, coax, or fiber patch cords) is fully seated at both the VoIP ATA and the wall termination point.
2. Log into the VoIP ATA administration portal (typically at 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1) using the credentials on the device label.
3. Navigate to the Status or Diagnostics page and check for active alarms, sync rate, or optical Rx/Tx power levels.
4. If using WiFi, confirm the client is connected to the correct SSID and band (2.4GHz vs 5GHz) for the required range and throughput.
5. Power cycle the VoIP ATA by unplugging it for 30 seconds, since this clears temporary DHCP, PPPoE, and firmware state issues.
6. If the issue persists, run a speed test via ethernet cable directly to the VoIP ATA to rule out WiFi interference.
