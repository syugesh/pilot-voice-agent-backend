# Historical Case: Case #100013 - Port forwarding not working

## Summary
Customer reported port forwarding not working on a Airtel 5G Home Internet connection using a Cable Modem. Case was resolved after 2 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and remote network diagnostics.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting port forwarding not working, which had been occurring for approximately 4 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was present at all times of day, including overnight.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed port forwarding not working via remote diagnostics on the Cable Modem.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be within normal range, pointing to a network-side cause.
- Customer-reported impact: streaming buffering and gaming lag.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for port forwarding not working on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by pushing a corrected firmware image after identifying the device had a failed OTA update. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 2 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
