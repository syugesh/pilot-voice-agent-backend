# Historical Case: Case #100021 - Slow download speeds

**Document ID:** CASE-0021

## Summary
Customer reported slow download speeds on a Comcast DSL connection using a Cable Modem. Case was resolved after 4 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and field dispatch.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting slow download speeds, which had been occurring for approximately 2 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was present at all times of day, including overnight.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed slow download speeds via remote diagnostics on the Cable Modem.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be within normal range, pointing to a network-side cause.
- Customer-reported impact: unable to work from home / VoIP calls dropping.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for slow download speeds on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by dispatching a field technician who found a corroded fiber connector at the NID and replaced the drop cable. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 4 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
