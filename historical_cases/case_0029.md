## Summary
Customer reported VPN tunnel not establishing on a BT Openreach Cable/DOCSIS connection using a Cable Modem. Case was resolved after 6 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and remote network diagnostics.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting VPN tunnel not establishing, which had been occurring for approximately 5 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was present at all times of day, including overnight.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed VPN tunnel not establishing via remote diagnostics on the Cable Modem.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be within normal range, pointing to a network-side cause.
- Customer-reported impact: smart home devices repeatedly disconnecting.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for VPN tunnel not establishing on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by re-provisioning the account after discovering a stale service profile from a prior address. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 6 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
