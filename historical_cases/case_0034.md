## Summary
Customer reported double NAT causing gaming issues on a Vodafone Fiber connection using a SFP Optical Module. Case was resolved after 5 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and remote network diagnostics.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting double NAT causing gaming issues, which had been occurring for approximately 5 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was worse during evening peak hours.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed double NAT causing gaming issues via remote diagnostics on the SFP Optical Module.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be outside normal operating parameters.
- Customer-reported impact: streaming buffering and gaming lag.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for double NAT causing gaming issues on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by isolating the issue to a customer-owned router in double-NAT and reconfiguring the ISP gateway to bridge mode. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 5 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
