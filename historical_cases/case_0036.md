## Summary
Customer reported fiber signal loss (Rx power low) on a Jio Fixed Wireless connection using a DSL Modem. Case was resolved after 1 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and field dispatch.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting fiber signal loss (Rx power low), which had been occurring for approximately 2 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was worse during evening peak hours.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed fiber signal loss (Rx power low) via remote diagnostics on the DSL Modem.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be outside normal operating parameters.
- Customer-reported impact: unable to work from home / VoIP calls dropping.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for fiber signal loss (Rx power low) on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by replacing a failing ONT that showed fluctuating optical Rx power outside spec. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 1 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
