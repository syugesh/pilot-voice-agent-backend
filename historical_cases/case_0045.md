# Historical Case: Case #100045 - DNS resolution errors

## Summary
Customer reported DNS resolution errors on a Vodafone DSL connection using a Cable Modem. Case was resolved after 4 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and field dispatch.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting DNS resolution errors, which had been occurring for approximately 1 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was present at all times of day, including overnight.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed DNS resolution errors via remote diagnostics on the Cable Modem.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be within normal range, pointing to a network-side cause.
- Customer-reported impact: unable to work from home / VoIP calls dropping.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for DNS resolution errors on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by confirming a regional fiber cut affecting the PON serving area and providing an ETA once splicing was complete. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 4 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
