# Historical Case: Case #100048 - VoIP call quality degradation

## Summary
Customer reported VoIP call quality degradation on a AT&T Fixed Wireless connection using a VoIP ATA. Case was resolved after 1 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and field dispatch.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting VoIP call quality degradation, which had been occurring for approximately 4 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was worse during evening peak hours.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed VoIP call quality degradation via remote diagnostics on the VoIP ATA.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be outside normal operating parameters.
- Customer-reported impact: unable to work from home / VoIP calls dropping.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for VoIP call quality degradation on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by identifying SIP ALG interference and disabling it on the gateway to resolve one-way audio. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 1 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
