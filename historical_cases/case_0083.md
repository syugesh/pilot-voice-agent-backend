## Summary
Customer reported PPPoE authentication failure on a Xfinity Cable/DOCSIS connection using a ONT. Case was resolved after 6 day(s) of investigation involving Tier 1, Tier 2, and remote network diagnostics.

## Problem Description
The customer contacted support reporting PPPoE authentication failure, which had been occurring for approximately 4 day(s) prior to the call. The customer noted the issue was present at all times of day, including overnight.

## Symptoms Observed
- Agent confirmed PPPoE authentication failure via remote diagnostics on the ONT.
- Signal/sync statistics were reviewed and found to be within normal range, pointing to a network-side cause.
- Customer-reported impact: smart home devices repeatedly disconnecting.

## Root Cause
Investigation determined the root cause to be consistent with known patterns for PPPoE authentication failure on this device class.

## Actions Taken / Resolution
The issue was ultimately resolved by pushing a corrected firmware image after identifying the device had a failed OTA update. The customer confirmed restored service with a follow-up test call 6 day(s) after the initial report, and no repeat tickets were logged in the following 30 days.
