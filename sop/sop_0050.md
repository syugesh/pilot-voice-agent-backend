# Standard Operating Procedure: New Customer Installation Provisioning

**Document ID:** SOP-0050
**Category:** SOP
**Department:** Field Operations
**Applicable Service:** Cisco TAC Cable/DOCSIS
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01
**Owner:** Cisco TAC Network Operations Center

## Summary
This SOP defines the mandatory step-by-step process for new customer installation provisioning at Cisco TAC, ensuring consistent, auditable, and safe execution across all regional support teams handling Cable/DOCSIS services.

## Scope
Applies to all Tier 1 and Tier 2 support agents, dispatch coordinators, and field technicians involved in new customer installation provisioning for residential and business Cable/DOCSIS subscribers.

## Prerequisites
- Agent must have active CRM and OSS system access with appropriate role permissions.
- Customer identity must be verified per the Identity Verification SOP prior to any account-impacting action.
- Field technicians must confirm van stock includes required Cable/DOCSIS equipment (patch cords, ONT units, splitters) before dispatch.

## Procedure
1. Open the customer's account record in the CRM and confirm the service address, account status, and current equipment inventory (Cable/DOCSIS CPE serial numbers).
2. Cross-check the request type against the eligibility matrix; new customer installation provisioning requests outside standard business hours require supervisor sign-off.
3. Log all actions taken in the account activity timeline, including timestamps and the agent or technician ID performing the action.
4. If provisioning changes are required on the network side (BNG, OLT, CMTS), submit a change request ticket through the OSS provisioning system and wait for confirmation before informing the customer.
5. Perform the technical action (installation, firmware rollback, suspension, RMA, etc.) following the relevant technical runbook.
6. Validate the outcome: for connectivity-related SOPs, confirm the device shows an online/synced state and run a basic throughput test; for account-related SOPs, confirm the account status reflects the change within 15 minutes.
7. Close the ticket with a resolution summary and set the appropriate disposition code for reporting purposes.

## Compliance Notes
All customer-facing actions must comply with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR for EU subscribers, TRAI guidelines for Indian subscribers). Any deviation from this SOP must be documented with a justification and approved by a shift supervisor.

## Escalation Rules
If the procedure cannot be completed due to a system outage, backend provisioning failure, or missing equipment, escalate to the Duty Manager and log a P3 internal ticket. Repeated failures (3+ in a shift) require escalation to Network Operations leadership.
